Introduction

Irene Roig Castellanos

I am training as a director while learning, project by project, how to work with a personal point of view and, at the same time, with different teams, rhythms and production methods. I come from Philosophy and have approached cinema from theory but also from shooting, production and archive work. I am interested in continuing to grow through different forms of filmmaking, moving between production, archival practices and personal projects.

05 About me

Irene Roig Castellanos

Graduated in Philosophy — University of Valencia / University of Bologna — and trained in film studies and audiovisual archive work. Currently pursuing the Master's in Cinema and Visual Culture Studies at ESCAC UB.

I have worked in cataloguing and conservation at the Filmoteca de Catalunya, and I participate in audiovisual projects and cultural programming.

My work as a director moves between fiction and documentary, with an attentive gaze on landscape and the everyday.

Although many of my pieces begin with observation, I want my master's training to help me master more industrial processes, work with larger teams and learn to direct any type of film or format I set out to create with versatility.
